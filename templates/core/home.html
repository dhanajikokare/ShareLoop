Trust & Accountability

Safety built into every handover

ShareLoop provides practical tools to protect both lenders and borrowers, ensuring every transaction is transparent and verifiable.

Photo Handover & Return Evidence

Both parties photograph and record the item's condition at pickup and return. These private logs protect you in case of any dispute.

Role-Based Transparency

Clear distinction between Individual Neighbors and Registered Business Shops. Verification badges are awarded only after administrative review.

Direct Dispute & Content Reporting

Report suspicious listings, fraudulent accounts, or non-returned items with one click. Our administrative moderation team reviews all reports.
