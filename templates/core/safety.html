Trust, Safety & Handover Verification

How we protect Pune's sharing community with accountability and real evidence

The Handover & Return Evidence System

At ShareLoop, we believe visual documentation prevents misunderstandings. For every rental, loan, or sale, authorized participants can upload timestamped photographs and condition logs.

Best Practices for Item Pickup:
  1. Inspect the item together during the physical meeting.
  2. Snap 2 to 3 photos showing key angles, accessories, and any pre-existing blemishes or scratches.
  3. Upload the photo to the transaction's Handover Log. Both parties can view this record privately.
Best Practices for Item Return:
  1. Verify that all parts, cords, cases, and accessories are intact.
  2. Power on electronic gear to confirm normal operation.
  3. Record the final return status. Once confirmed, security deposits are immediately eligible for release.

Private Data & Upload Protection

Evidence photos and handover notes are strictly protected by server-side object authorization. Only the specific transaction participants (lender & borrower, or buyer & seller) and authorized administrative moderators have access to private handover records.

Reporting Suspicious Activity

Every product listing and user profile features an easy-to-use Report button. If you spot counterfeit goods, fraudulent rental pricing, or inappropriate behavior, our administrative team reviews all submissions within 24 hours.

Meeting in Pune

When meeting to handover items, prefer well-lit, public locations like Metro stations (Vanaz, Garware College, Nal Stop, Pune Station), popular cafes, or apartment security lobbies.

Emergency Helpline: Dial 112
Community Rules
  • No hazardous, flammable, or prohibited weapons.
  • Never ask for off-platform upfront cash transfers without proof.
  • Always communicate politely and respect neighbors' time.
