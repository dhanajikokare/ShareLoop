Terms and Conditions of Use

Last updated: October 2026 | Applicable Law: Republic of India

1. Introduction & Platform Role

ShareLoop operates an online peer-to-peer and business-to-consumer marketplace connecting lenders, renters, borrowers, and buyers in Pune, Maharashtra. ShareLoop provides technical facilitation and verification infrastructure.

2. Marketplace Modes & Free Borrow Guarantee

Users may list items for Rent, Borrow, or Sell. Listings marked as 'Borrow' are strictly free of rental fees. Any refundable security deposit must be explicitly disclosed and agreed prior to handover.

3. Handover Responsibility & Condition Evidence

Participants are responsible for inspecting items upon pickup and return. The Handover Evidence system serves as the definitive reference in resolving disputes.

4. Prohibited Items

Illegal goods, prescription drugs, explosives, firearms, and items infringing intellectual property are strictly forbidden. Infringing listings will be removed immediately and accounts reported to relevant authorities.

5. Limitation of Liability

ShareLoop facilitates transactions but is not an insurer of physical property. Users are encouraged to maintain appropriate personal or commercial insurance coverage.
