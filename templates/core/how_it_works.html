How ShareLoop Works

Pune's community platform for renting, lending, and buying goods sustainably

Paid Rental Model

Renting Products On ShareLoop

Renting allows owners to monetize underutilized high-value items (cameras, tools, electronics, event supplies) while providing renters with affordable short-term access.

1
Select Dates & Instant Cost Preview

Choose your start and return dates. The system verifies real-time calendar availability, calculates daily rates, refundable security deposit, and transparent platform commission.

2
Owner Review & Acceptance

The owner receives an instant notification with your rental duration and message. Owners can accept or decline according to their schedule.

3
Payment & Photo Handover Log

Upon acceptance, secure payment is completed. At pickup, both parties log the item's condition with photo evidence in the private Handover Log.

4
Return, Deposit Release & Review

Return the item in agreed condition, log the return evidence, release the security deposit, and exchange authentic community reviews.

Rental Transparency Rules
  • Zero Overlapping Bookings: The system locks calendar dates automatically to prevent duplicate or conflict reservations.
  • Deposits Are Protected: Refundable security deposits are never treated as revenue and are reconciled upon return.
  • Cancellation Policies: Disclosed upfront before any booking request is submitted.

Community Goodwill

100% Free Peer Borrowing

Borrowing is built entirely on neighborhood trust and mutual aid. Perfect for books, study guides, simple DIY hand tools, sports gear, and camping equipment.

1
Zero Rental Fees Guaranteed

Borrow listings are strictly free of rental fees. If an owner requires an optional refundable deposit, it is transparently stated upfront.

2
Request & Agree to Care Terms

Select your borrowing days and share a respectful message explaining how you will care for the item. Agree to return on time.

3
Friendly Local Pickup & Return

Meet your neighbor in Pune, record the handover note, and return the item cleanly on or before the agreed date.

The Borrowing Code

"Never convert a free loan into a transaction." Borrowing builds social capital in Pune. Members who fail to return borrowed items or mistreat items face immediate account suspension.

Direct Local Commerce

Buy & Sell Products Securely

Give items a second life or sell inventory directly from your local shop or home in Pune.

1
Real-Time Inventory Lock

When a buyer places an order, the system locks and deducts inventory safely using database transactions to prevent overselling.

2
Pickup or Delivery Coordination

Buyers select self-pickup at the seller's Pune locality or coordinate direct delivery.

3
Order Status Tracking & Reviews

Track order statuses (Confirmed, Processing, Ready for Pickup, Completed) and review the transaction upon handover.

Inventory Integrity

A sold item cannot remain available for new rentals or borrowing unless the seller has distinct physical units in stock.
