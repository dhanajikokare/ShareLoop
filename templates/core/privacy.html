Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | ShareLoop India

1. Information We Collect

We collect account information (name, email address, phone number, Pune locality) required to facilitate marketplace trust. We do not track or store your exact GPS location without explicit permission.

2. Transaction & Evidence Privacy

Handover photographs and private notes are accessible strictly by the parties engaged in that specific transaction and administrative moderators handling disputes.

3. Payment Information Security

ShareLoop never stores sensitive payment credentials, debit/credit card numbers, or CVVs. All electronic transactions are processed through RBI-authorized payment aggregators (such as Razorpay) complying with PCI-DSS standards.

4. Your Data Rights

You can view, edit, or request deletion of your account and personal listings at any time through your Profile Dashboard or by contacting support.
